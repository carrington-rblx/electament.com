Bio

AmentAmour

AmentAmour established a reputation as a masterful diplomat and one of the nation's most widely praised Secretaries of State.

Serving as the 18th Secretary of State following key roles on Senate Foreign Relations and Intelligence, his tenure remains celebrated for steady executive leadership and global statecraft.
