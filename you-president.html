The future is a choice

What would you do if you were President?

You President is an interactive public-policy experience. Across 25 randomly selected proposals, you decide what should be rejected, improved, kept, or left undecided. Your answers create a personal snapshot of the priorities that matter to you.

How it works

25 proposals. 4 ways to respond.

01 / REJECT

I Reject

Don't support the proposal? Reject it and move on to the next policy.

02 / IMPROVE

I Improve

See a better way? Suggest a short improvement before continuing.

03 / KEEP

I Keep · I Don't Know

Keep a proposal you support, or choose I Don't Know when you don't have a view.
